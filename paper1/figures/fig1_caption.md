# Figure 1 — caption

**Figure 1.** ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 140-2023, Section 7 annual heating and
cooling acceptance comparisons for the EnergyPlus v26.1 WebAssembly build:
(a) the low-mass group and (b) the high-mass group. Horizontal grey segments
show the inclusive Annex A3 acceptance ranges; blue circles and orange diamonds
show the WebAssembly heating and cooling results, respectively. Labels without
a subtraction are absolute annual loads (Cases 600 and 900); the remaining
labels are the prescribed differences between cases. All 21 low-mass
comparisons are within range (minimum required: 18), and all 19 high-mass
comparisons are within range (minimum required: 17). These results establish
that the tested build meets the Standard 140-2023-as-published Section 7 annual
acceptance criteria; they do not constitute an unqualified whole-standard
compliance claim or include the time-series criteria later introduced by
Addendum a.
